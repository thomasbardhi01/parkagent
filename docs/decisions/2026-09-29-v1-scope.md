## Decisions

Each has one line of rationale.

### Street parking

1. **Street parking is fully automatic, with a tap to confirm that shows
   the amount.** Detection, zone, quote, and payment need no typing. The
   one interruption is a notification that says what it will pay ("Pay
   $4.10 for zone 456, 1 h 05 m?"), and one tap confirms it.
   *Rationale:* a price the user saw is the only automatic payment the user
   can't be surprised by, and it costs one tap.

2. **A session starts when the phone leaves the car and ends when it
   returns.** The paid session begins at walk-away, not at the moment of
   the stop. Coming back to the car ends it: stopped at the provider where
   early stop exists, and no further extensions where it doesn't
   (ParkBoston).
   Nothing prompts or extends while the driver is sitting in the car, so
   the tap-to-confirm arrives at walk-away. An early departure and a
   re-park start a fresh detection (confirmed 2026-09-30).
   *Rationale:* the phone leaving the car is the moment parking actually
   starts, and the phone returning is the moment it can safely end.

3. **Auto-extend near max stay.** Extension stays automatic as a session
   nears expiry, up to the zone's max stay and never past it. Near the max
   stay the driver is told to move the car.
   *Rationale:* a ticket at a meter we were paying is the worst outcome
   the product can produce, and the max stay is the one limit money can't
   buy past.

4. **YOLO mode is a friends-and-family beta.** It's off by default for
   everyone, and only an admin can put someone on the allowlist. An
   allowlisted user opts in through a consent screen whose beta wording
   says payments may happen without a tap and may occasionally be wrong,
   and can turn it off with one tap. Every automatic payment runs through
   the hard caps, the user's own limits, and the beta test caps; anything
   over a cap falls back to tap-to-confirm. Every automatic payment writes
   a decisions row recording it was made under YOLO mode (confirmed
   2026-09-30).
   *Rationale:* we learn whether no-tap parking is wanted from people who
   will tell us when it's wrong, without offering it to strangers.

### Garages

5. **Garages in V1 are SpotHero/ParkWhiz pass-through in an in-app
   browser.** The user checks out on the marketplace's own page inside the
   app (`SFSafariViewController`). ParkAgent never automates a garage
   checkout and never injects JavaScript into one.
   *Rationale:* garage checkout has no linked account and no partner
   agreement behind it, so the user must be the one who pays.

6. **Read-only public garage search is allowed under explicit modes and a
   daily budget.** Each garage source runs `off`, `public`, or `partner`,
   and a public reader stops at its per-day request budget.
   *Rationale:* reading public search at low volume is how garages appear
   at all until a partner key arrives, and a mode plus a budget keeps it
   deliberate and bounded.

7. **Garage prices show when they were fetched, and an admin view tracks
   freshness.** Every garage price on a card says "as of 3:42 PM". An
   admin view shows how old the cached prices are per source.
   *Rationale:* a scraped price is a claim about the past, and the user and
   we should both see how far in the past.

### The assistant

8. **Always honor the user's explicit ask.** Closest-vs-cheapest
   alternatives are shown as secondary. When the user gave no constraint,
   both are offered.
   *Rationale:* the reported bug is an assistant that drifts from what was
   asked, and showing the other axis second keeps the user informed without
   overriding them.

### Money

9. **Per-trip caps are dropped for individuals; the daily cap is kept;
   beta users get test caps.** An individual no longer sets a per-stop or
   per-plan cap. Every account keeps its daily cap. Beta testers run under
   low test caps: $15 a day and $10 a stop, adjustable by an admin in
   `policy.json`'s `beta_caps` (confirmed 2026-09-30). `policy.json`'s `session_cap_usd` stays
   only as the system's own ceiling against a misquote.
   *Rationale:* a per-trip cap mostly blocked legitimate long stays, while
   the daily cap bounds real exposure.

### Deferred to V2

10. **Deferred:**
    - ticket capture and garage stays (park-now PRs 4–6: the scanner and
      parser, stays with rate cards and a Live Activity, and the stay
      budget on the Issuing webhook);
    - valet mode;
    - B2B;
    - rental cars;
    - per-trip caps.

    *Rationale:* each needs a partner, a legal read, or field data we don't
    have yet, and none blocks a driver paying a Boston meter.

decisions answer: per-plan cap default (report 1, Q6) is moot; garage
refusal on stale data (report 1, Q5) becomes "show the fetched time"
(decision 7); the detector's auto-pay threshold (report 1, Q1) is the
tap-to-confirm (decision 1).
